What Sibr checks

Six parameters, one Sibr score out of 100
PainDoes the problem really hurt? Strong pain means people already spend time or money on workarounds.
ValueWill customers pay for the solution, and how much?
Target audienceIs there a clear first customer? A sharp target is cheaper to reach and easier to build for.
GrowthIs the market growing, steady or shrinking?
Islamic alignmentIs this a business a Muslim founder can run? Looks for riba, gambling, haram categories, speculation and content concerns. Your customers can be anyone. A flag for scholarly review, never a ruling.
CompetitionIs the space open, competitive with room to stand out, or crowded?

Sibr score = 65% the first four parameters, 20% competition, 15% Islamic alignment. Each idea is also scored for its audiences: ones Sibr finds for you, ones you pick from 141 segments, or ones you type.

How the engine works

  1. Idea cardProblem, user, solution, revenue, delivery.
  2. Lens gridGeography × age × community × interest cells.
  3. EvidenceCompetitors, reviews, forums, trends per cell.
  4. Persona passLLM writes a short analysis per cell.
  5. Verdict headsFine-tuned Laya classifiers return typed, calibrated verdicts.

With the model service connected, ideas are scored by an LLM idea card plus Laya verdicts. Without it, a transparent heuristic engine runs in the browser. Scores are directional, not research. Islamic-alignment output is a flag for scholarly review, never a ruling.
