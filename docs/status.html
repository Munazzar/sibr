Where Sibr is, and what comes next

The website, the Laya service on your PC, the logs and the guides are built and live. Next comes training: teaching Laya to judge ideas the way you do, then checking it against your own grades before it goes live. All of it runs free on your own computer.

3 in progress7 coming next19 done

What it all produces at the end

  1. Anyone can paste a business idea, of any length, and get a Sibr score out of 100 with a plain verdict (Strong, Promising, Risky, Weak or Flagged).
  2. Each of the six parameters is explained: what Laya chose, how sure it was, and the odds for each level.
  3. The best audiences for the idea, found automatically or picked from 141 segments or typed in, each with a fit score and a one-line reason.
  4. Web evidence (competitors, Reddit threads, alternatives) and every step's timing, shown behind the scenes.
  5. After training: a Laya model fine-tuned on your own grades, so its verdicts match how you judge ideas. All of it runs free on your PC.

What you need to do

Five steps, in this order. Everything else on this page runs by itself.

  1. Leave the PC on tonight, and don't play games on itTraining uses the graphics card. A game makes it about five times slower.
  2. Grade the gold set: about 150 ideasWhen you're told it's ready, open server\training\data\gold.csv in Excel. For each idea, fill in pain, value, target and growth (0 = low, 1 = medium, 2 = high), plus competition and Islamic alignment. The first row lists the allowed answers. Leave a cell blank if you're unsure. It takes about an hour, and it can be done over several sittings.
  3. Have the Islamic column checkedIf you can, ask someone with knowledge of Islamic rulings to look over that one column. A model's opinion isn't enough there.
  4. Optional: skim the graded datasetOpen server\training\data\review.csv. Fix any grade that looks wrong, or set keep to 0 to drop a row.
  5. Read the before/after report and approve the switchIt shows, for each of the six checks, how often the old and new Laya agree with your grades. The new model goes live only after you say yes.
